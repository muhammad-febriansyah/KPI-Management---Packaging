# Views Realizations

## Realization fields are optional
The Realisasi Pekerjaan form follows the requirement table: Tanggal Borongan, Shift, Nomor Batch, SKU, product snapshot, total output, work times, report, and employee assignment are all optional. Superadmin may save an empty/partial realization; assigning employees is optional and can be completed later.
